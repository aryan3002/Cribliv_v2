# Cribliv — Verified rentals in North India

Cribliv is an AI-powered rental discovery platform focused on Tier-1 and Tier-2
North Indian cities. Every listing is owner-verified, and tenants get a
12-hour refund guarantee on confirmed bookings.

## What you can do here

- Browse verified flats, PGs, and houses in **Delhi, Gurugram, Noida,
  Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, and Lucknow**.
- Filter by budget, BHK, locality, intent (working professional, student, family),
  and proximity to metro stations or landmarks.
- Contact owners directly without brokers — once a tenant is verified.
