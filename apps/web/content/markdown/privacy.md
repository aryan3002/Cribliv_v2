# Privacy

Cribliv collects the minimum personal data needed to verify identity and run
the rental marketplace.

## What we collect

- Phone number (for OTP login)
- Government ID + property documents (owners only, encrypted at rest)
- Search and click history (used to rank listings; not sold)

## What we don't do

- We don't sell personal data.
- We don't allow training of third-party AI models on tenant or owner PII.
  See our `Content-Signal: ai-train=no` directive in `/robots.txt`.
- We don't share contact information until both parties consent.

For the full policy, see the live page at `/en/privacy`.
