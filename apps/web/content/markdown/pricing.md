# Pricing

## Tenants

**Free.** Searching, shortlisting, contacting verified owners, and booking visits
cost nothing. The 12-hour refund guarantee is included on every confirmed
booking.

## Owners

- **Free** to list — unlimited listings.
- **Verification** is free.
- **Boosts** (optional) — pay-per-boost to surface higher in city + locality
  search. Pricing is shown at the time of purchase and varies by city.

## PG operators

- Free to list and manage units, residents, and rent collection.
- Optional subscription tier unlocks priority search, advanced analytics, and
  multi-property dashboards.

For partner / agent access, see `partners@cribliv.com`.
