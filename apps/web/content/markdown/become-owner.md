# Become an owner on Cribliv

List your property for free. Get verified leads from phone-verified tenants.

## Steps

1. Sign up via phone OTP at `/en/auth/login`.
2. Choose role: **Owner** or **PG Operator**.
3. Upload property details (address, BHK, photos, rent, deposit).
4. Verify ownership: Aadhaar + property document.
5. Listing goes live as "Verified" within 24 hours.

## What we ask

- Genuine ownership documentation
- Accurate photos (we use AI to detect stock-image abuse)
- Honest rent and deposit numbers (we benchmark against locality medians)

## Optional

- **Boosts** to surface higher in city/locality search
- **PG operator subscription** for multi-property dashboards
