# How Cribliv works

## For tenants

1. **Search**. Use `/en/search` or browse a city hub `/en/city/{slug}`. Filter by
   budget, BHK, locality, and intent.
2. **Shortlist**. Save listings to your shortlist (requires login via phone OTP).
3. **Contact owner**. Once verified, contact the owner directly — no brokers.
4. **Book a visit**. Schedule visits in-app with a refundable hold.
5. **Refund guarantee**. Cancel within 12 hours of booking for a full refund.

## For owners

1. **List for free**. `/en/become-owner` to start.
2. **Verify ownership**. Aadhaar + property document upload.
3. **Receive verified leads**. Tenants are phone-verified before contact.
4. **Optional boost**. Pay-per-boost to surface higher in search.

## Pricing

Tenants: free. Owners: free to list, optional paid boosts. See `/en/pricing`.
